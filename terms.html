What SouthHop does

SouthHop shows one-way fares between Auckland and Dunedin that other websites have found. It's a free information service. We aren't a travel agent or an airline, we don't sell tickets, and we don't take payments.

Prices are a guide only

Prices come from a third-party data feed of recent searches. They can be several days old, and fares change often. A price on SouthHop isn't an offer to sell you a ticket. The price, baggage allowance and fare rules on the booking website are the ones that apply.

Bookings on other websites

When you book through a link from SouthHop, your contract is with the airline or booking website, not with us. Their terms, privacy policy and refund rules apply. Questions about a booking need to go to them.

How we're paid

Some fare links include a Travelpayouts partner code. If you book after following one, we may earn a commission. This doesn't change the price you pay or the order of fares, which are always sorted by price.

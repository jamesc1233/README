Cookie policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

SouthHop doesn't use cookies. This page explains what that covers and what happens when you visit other websites from here.

What we store on your device

Nothing. SouthHop doesn't set cookies, and doesn't use local storage, analytics, advertising pixels, social media widgets or other tracking tools.

Our fonts, images and scripts are served from this website. We don't embed maps, videos or content from other websites, so they can't set cookies through our pages either.

Why there's no cookie banner

Cookie banners ask for permission to store cookies or tracking data that a website doesn't strictly need. SouthHop stores none, so there's nothing to ask about. If we ever add something that needs your permission, we'll ask before it runs and update this page.

Cookies on other websites

When you follow a link to another website, such as Aviasales, Google Flights or Skyscanner, that website may set its own cookies. If a fare link includes our Travelpayouts partner code, Travelpayouts or Aviasales may set a cookie on their own website so they can tell us about a booking. Those cookies are covered by those websites' own policies.

Our web host

GitHub, which hosts SouthHop, records visitors' IP addresses for security. It doesn't use cookies for this. See our privacy policy for details.

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information SouthHop collects, why we collect it, and your rights under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. SouthHop is run by {{BUSINESS_NAME}} ("we", "us"), {{CITY}}, New Zealand.

Summary

  • You can search fares without giving us any personal information.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • If you email us, we use your details only to reply to you.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information.

When you visit the site

SouthHop is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. GitHub records the IP address of every visitor for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don't receive or have access to these records.

Our fonts, images and scripts are all served from this website. Your browser doesn't contact Google or any other third party just because you opened a page here.

When you contact us

Our contact form doesn't send anything to us by itself. It opens a new email in your own email app. If you send that email, we receive your name, your email address, your message, and anything else your email includes.

We use this information only to read and reply to your message. We keep the email for up to 12 months after our conversation ends, then delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. Our emails are stored by {{EMAIL_PROVIDER}}, which may store them outside New Zealand.

When you follow a fare link

Fare links take you to other websites, such as Aviasales, Google Flights and Skyscanner. Those websites collect information under their own privacy policies, so please read them before you book.

Some fare links include a partner code so that Travelpayouts can tell us when a booking came from SouthHop. To do this, Travelpayouts or Aviasales may set a cookie on their own website after you arrive there. We don't receive your name, contact details or payment details from them.

Fare data

The prices we show come from the Travelpayouts data feed. It contains fares, dates and flight numbers only. It doesn't contain any information about the people who searched.

Who we share information with

We only share personal information when we need to reply to you (through our email provider), when the law requires it, or with your permission.

Keeping information safe

We keep emails in a password-protected email account and delete them when we no longer need them. We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss and misuse.

Refund policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

SouthHop doesn't sell tickets or take payments, so there's nothing for us to refund. This page explains where to go if you need a refund for a flight.

Refunds for flights

If you booked a flight after following a link from SouthHop, you paid the airline or the booking website, and they hold your booking. Contact them about refunds, changes and cancellations. Their fare rules decide what you can get back. Many of the cheapest fares can't be refunded, so check before you book.

Your consumer rights

New Zealand consumer law, including the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, may give you rights against the business that sold you the flight, whatever its own policy says. Consumer Protection explains these rights and how to resolve a problem.

Problems with SouthHop

If a price or link on SouthHop was wrong, please tell us through the contact page so we can fix it. Because you didn't pay us, we can't refund the difference, but the booking website should show you the real price before you pay.

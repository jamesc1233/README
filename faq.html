Questions and answers

Quick answers about how SouthHop works. Select a question to open it.

Where do the prices come from?

From the Travelpayouts data feed, which stores fares that people found when they searched on Aviasales in the last few days. We check it about every 6 hours. We don't get prices directly from the airlines.

Why is the price different when I click through?

Fares change often, and the price we show may be a few days old. Seats at the lowest price can also sell out. The price on the booking site is the one you'll pay, so always check it before you book.

Can I book a flight on SouthHop?

No. SouthHop doesn't sell tickets or take payments. Fare links take you to another website, and you book with that website or the airline.

How does SouthHop make money?

Fare links can include a partner code from Travelpayouts. If you book after following one, we may earn a small commission from them. You pay the same price either way, and it doesn't change which fares we show: the list is always sorted by price.

Are these one-way or return fares?

One-way. For a return trip, check the fare in each direction. Use the swap button between AKL and DUD on the boarding pass to change direction.

Does the price include checked baggage?

Not always. Many low fares only include carry-on bags. The booking site shows what's included before you pay.

How up to date are the prices?

The fares page shows when the prices were last updated. If they are more than a day old, the page says so. Prices in the feed can be up to a week old, even right after an update.

Do you use cookies or track me?

No. SouthHop doesn't set cookies, run analytics or use advertising trackers. See the cookie policy and privacy policy.

How do I get a refund or change a booking?

Contact the airline or the website you booked with. They have your booking and payment. Our refund policy explains more.

How do I contact you?

Use the contact page. We can't make or change bookings, but we're happy to hear about mistakes on the site.

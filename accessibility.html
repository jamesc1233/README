Accessibility

Last updated 10 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use SouthHop, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or high-contrast settings.

Our goal

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Every page has a "Skip to main content" link, headings in order, and labelled menus.
  • You can use everything with a keyboard, with a clear outline showing where you are.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG AA colour-contrast levels in light and dark mode.
  • Every form field has a label, and error messages say what to fix.
  • Animations switch off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The price chart is also available as a text list of the cheapest days.

Known limits

Fare details come from a third-party feed, and the websites we link to have their own accessibility, which we can't control.
